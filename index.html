How this works

  1. Score every abstract from 1 to 5. Tap the numbers, or press the number keys. Pressing the same number again clears the score.
  2. Full talks and egg-timers are scored separately. Switch with the tabs at the top and work through both.
  3. Move between abstracts with the index list, or the ↑ and ↓ keys on a computer.
  4. Notes are optional and go to the organizer.
  5. My list shows the current track in your own order. Reordering with the arrows, or by dragging on a computer, is optional and helps break ties between abstracts you gave the same score.
  6. Everything saves as you go, on any device. Scores can be changed until scoring closes.
